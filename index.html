About Us

CoreEmbed Technologies is an embedded systems engineering company focused on delivering reliable, high-performance hardware and software solutions. We specialize in deep-tech development where firmware, hardware, operating systems, and real-time constraints meet.

From bare-metal firmware to Linux kernel drivers and FPGA-based designs, we help startups, OEMs, and enterprises build robust products faster and with confidence.
